Retail Security

Loss prevention and uniformed store security helping retailers minimise theft while keeping stores welcoming.

Service Overview

Retail theft and staff safety are major concerns for high-street stores and shopping centres. ORYA Security officers provide a visible loss prevention presence, polite customer engagement, and theft response handled in line with UK law.

Client Operational Benefits

Helps reduce retail stock shrinkage through visible deterrence
Reassures store staff and supports a safe shopping atmosphere
Deters organised retail crime and repeat offenders
Professional incident documentation to support any police involvement
